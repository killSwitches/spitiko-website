Built for a proper night's rest

Ora Lodge sits on a quiet plot in Ga-Masemola, made up of separate thatched cottages rather than a single block of rooms. Rooms are finished with tiled or marble floors, ensuite bathrooms, and proper beds with real headboards — not an afterthought.

The lodge is designed for a quiet, comfortable stay, with ensuite rooms, homely touches, and on-site parking for guests.

About Ora Lodge

A small lodge, run in person

Ora Lodge is a guesthouse in Ga-Masemola, Limpopo, made up of a handful of thatched cottages rather than a large hotel block. The lodge is run in person, with a focus on comfortable rooms and a relaxed countryside setting.

Rooms are finished individually and include ensuite bathrooms. The property also has on-site parking for guest vehicles.

The property is straightforward to find and has on-site parking for guest vehicles.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Who we are

This policy explains how Ora Lodge ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information belonging to visitors of this website and guests who contact us. Ora Lodge is based in Ga-Masemola, Limpopo, South Africa, and this policy is written with reference to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us directly, for example through the contact form on this site. This may include your name, email address, phone number, preferred travel dates, and any other details you include in a message to us.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, or third-party embeds on this website, so we do not automatically collect browsing data, device identifiers, or location data through the site itself.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and booking requests.
  • To communicate with you about a stay you have booked or asked about.
  • To keep basic records required for running the lodge, such as booking confirmations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where required by law or necessary to fulfil a booking you have requested.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage your stay and meet any legal or accounting requirements, after which it is deleted or securely archived.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to request that it be corrected, and to request that it be deleted where we are not required to keep it. To make a request, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
